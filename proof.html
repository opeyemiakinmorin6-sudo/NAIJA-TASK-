Understand your options

Pending: Your proof is waiting for a decision. Avoid submitting the same task repeatedly while it is under review.
Rejected: Read the rejection reason. If retries are allowed and the task remains available, open it again and submit corrected proof.
Request review: Use this if you believe a rejection was unfair. Explain why your original proof meets the task requirements.
Approved: Your submission was accepted. Your actual wallet balance depends on the platform's secure reward-processing system.
