How proof review works: Open a submission, inspect the original task link and evidence, then tick the confirmation box before approving. Genuine approvals are normally paid immediately through the secure database function. Credible security warnings require administrator review before payout; inconclusive automated checks do not automatically label a Tasker as fraudulent. Rejection requires a clear reason and gives the Tasker appeal rights. Posters can review only their own funded tasks; admins handle escalations, appeals and fraud review.
