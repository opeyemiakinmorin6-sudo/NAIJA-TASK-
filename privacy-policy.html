Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how NAIJA TASK handles information when you visit or use the website and services. It applies to Taskers, Posters and visitors.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide, such as name, email address, phone number, state, city/town, account role and referral information.
  • Authentication and session information processed by our authentication provider. Never send us your password or one-time security codes.
  • Platform content and activity, including jobs, listings, task submissions, uploaded proof, orders and support requests.
  • Payment records such as transaction references, amounts and payment status needed to reconcile payments, balances and withdrawals. Checkout credentials are handled by the payment provider; do not send card numbers or banking PINs to support.
  • Technical, device, browser, log and usage information needed for security and service operation.

How information is used

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide task and marketplace features, process and reconcile payments, review submissions, prevent fraud, respond to support requests, improve the service and meet legal obligations.

Service providers and sharing

We may use Supabase for database and authentication services, Flutterwave for payment processing, and Google services such as AdSense if advertising is enabled. Providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the platform, or in a legitimate business transfer.

Advertising, cookies and similar technologies

Eligible public pages may display Google AdSense advertisements. If ads are enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to store or access browser information, measure advertising and, where permitted by your settings and applicable requirements, personalise ads. Manage ad personalisation through Google account ad settings and cookies through your browser. We never require users to click advertisements as a task. Essential storage may be used for account and site functions.

Public content and external links

Content you submit as a task, listing or other public contribution may be visible to other users. Do not include private information unless genuinely required. External websites have their own privacy policies.

Security, retention and your choices

We use measures intended to protect information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to operate the service, resolve disputes, maintain financial and security records and comply with law. You may contact us to ask about, correct or request deletion of personal information, subject to identity checks and records we must retain.

Children

NAIJA TASK is not designed for children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. For privacy questions, email naijataskofficia@gmail.com or contact Customer Care on WhatsApp.
