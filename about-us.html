About NAIJA TASK

NAIJA TASK is a Nigerian digital task and job marketplace created to connect people who need digital work completed with people willing to complete those tasks.

Our goal is to make online tasks clearer, more organised and easier to manage.

Our Story

The internet has created many opportunities for people to complete digital jobs, promote businesses, perform online activities, provide feedback and complete other assignments.

But finding suitable tasks and managing them properly can be difficult. Posters need people who can follow instructions, while Taskers need clear information about what they are expected to do.

NAIJA TASK was created to bring these two sides together through one organised platform.

Our goal: provide a structured environment where jobs can be posted, tasks can be completed, proof can be submitted and work can be reviewed.

Who We Are

NAIJA TASK is a digital platform focused on connecting Taskers and Posters through organised online work.

We are building the platform around simplicity, transparency, responsible task management and a better experience for Nigerian users.

As the platform grows, we will continue improving the tools, features and systems that make digital work easier to manage.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make digital work more accessible and organised by giving Taskers and Posters a platform where instructions, submissions and job activity can be clearly managed.

For Taskers

Discover available tasks, understand requirements, submit proof and track task activity.

For Posters

Create jobs, provide instructions, fund campaigns and manage submissions.

For Businesses

Organise approved digital activities and connect with people who can complete them.

For Digital Workers

Participate in structured digital assignments and complete approved online work.

Our Vision

We want NAIJA TASK to grow into a Nigerian digital marketplace where online jobs and micro-tasks can be organised in a way that is understandable to both sides of the marketplace.

The long-term vision is to make the platform useful for individuals, creators, small businesses and organisations that need digital work completed.

For Taskers

Taskers are people who complete available jobs on the platform.

Tasks may involve visiting websites, following instructions, interacting with supported platforms, answering surveys, testing something or completing another approved digital activity.

Taskers should always read the complete task instructions before starting.

For Posters

Posters are people, creators or businesses that need digital work completed.

Posters can create jobs containing information such as the title, description, instructions, task link, reward, available slots and proof requirements.

Jobs follow an organised funding, review and publication process before becoming available to Taskers.

Safety, Verification & Fairness

Trust is important to a task marketplace. NAIJA TASK is designed with systems intended to help reduce fake submissions, duplicate activity and misuse of the platform.

Depending on the task, proof may include photographs, screenshots, usernames, comments, links, answers or other information requested by the Poster.

Task activity and submissions can be reviewed before rewards are credited.

Important: Never provide passwords, authentication codes, banking PINs or other private security credentials as task proof.
