About NAIJA TASK

NAIJA TASK is a task marketplace designed to connect Taskers with people and businesses that need tasks completed.

What We Do

NAIJA TASK provides a platform where Taskers can discover available tasks, follow instructions, submit proof of completion and receive rewards when their work is approved.

Posters can create jobs, set task requirements, provide rewards and reach Taskers who are available to complete their tasks.

For Taskers

Taskers can use NAIJA TASK to:

  • Find available tasks.
  • Follow task instructions.
  • Submit the required proof.
  • Track submitted work.
  • Receive rewards after approved completion.

For Posters

Posters can use NAIJA TASK to:

  • Create and publish task jobs.
  • Set rewards and available slots.
  • Add task instructions and requirements.
  • Receive submitted proof from Taskers.
  • Manage their jobs through the Poster Dashboard.

Our Approach

We aim to provide a clear and structured environment for task-based work, with task instructions, proof submission, job status and payment processes kept separate and easy to understand.

Safety & Fairness

Taskers should complete tasks according to the instructions provided by Posters. Posters should provide accurate task descriptions and valid requirements.

NAIJA TASK may review submitted proof, task activity and account activity where necessary to help maintain the platform.
