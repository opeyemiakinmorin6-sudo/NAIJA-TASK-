ABOUT NAIJA TASK

A simpler way to organise digital work.

NAIJA TASK is a Nigerian digital task marketplace built to connect people who need online work completed with people ready to complete it. We bring job instructions, submissions and review activity into one organised experience.

Choose your account →Contact support
WHY WE EXIST

Good work starts with clear expectations.

Finding people to complete digital assignments can be difficult when instructions, proof and feedback are scattered. NAIJA TASK is designed to give Taskers and Posters a shared place to organise that process.

Our focus: clear job details, understandable task requirements, organised submissions and visible account activity.
🎯

Our mission

Make digital task management more accessible and straightforward for Nigerian individuals, creators and businesses—while helping Taskers understand what is expected before they begin.


🌱

Our direction

Continue improving the platform's task tools, member experience, support and payment processes as services become available and verified.

HOW IT WORKS

From job posting to task review.

The platform is organised around a straightforward workflow.

01

Join

Create a Tasker account to complete jobs or a Poster account to publish jobs.

02

Find or publish

Taskers browse available tasks. Posters prepare job details and required funding.

03

Complete and submit

Taskers follow the instructions and provide the requested proof.

04

Review and track

Submissions are reviewed against the task requirements and activity can be tracked.

WHO THE PLATFORM SERVES

Two account types. One organised workflow.

👤

For Taskers

Explore available digital jobs, read the requirements, complete the requested work, submit proof and follow submission and wallet activity.

🏢

For Posters

Create job listings, define instructions and rewards, provide required funding and review submissions received from Taskers.

RESPONSIBLE USE

Safety and transparency matter.

Members should read task requirements carefully and use the platform responsibly. Task approval and reward processing depend on the applicable task rules and review outcome.

Protect your account: NAIJA TASK will never need your password, one-time verification code or banking PIN as task proof. Do not share these details with another member.
