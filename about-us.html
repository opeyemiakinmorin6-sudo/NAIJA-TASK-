About NAIJA TASK

NAIJA TASK is a Nigerian digital task and job marketplace created to connect people who need digital work completed with people who are willing to complete those tasks.

The platform is designed around a simple idea: make online tasks clearer, more organised and easier to manage.

Our Story

The internet has created many opportunities for people to complete small digital jobs, promote businesses, perform social-media activities, test services, provide feedback and complete other online assignments.

However, finding genuine tasks and managing those tasks properly can sometimes be difficult. Posters need people who can follow instructions, while Taskers need clear information about what they are expected to do.

NAIJA TASK was created to bring these two sides together through one organised platform.

The goal: create a structured environment where jobs can be posted, tasks can be completed, proof can be submitted and rewards can be reviewed through an organised process.

Founder & Builder — DAVELEX

NAIJA TASK was founded and built under the DAVELEX brand with a simple idea: create a Nigerian digital marketplace where people can connect through legitimate online tasks and organised digital work.

The platform began with the belief that small digital activities can create value when they are properly organised, clearly explained and responsibly managed.

The vision behind NAIJA TASK is to build more than a task website. It is to create a platform where Taskers, businesses, creators and digital communities can interact through a structured system for jobs, proof, review and rewards.

As the platform develops, the focus remains on useful technology, better organisation, transparency and a better experience for Nigerian users.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make digital work more accessible and organised by giving Taskers and Posters a platform where expectations, instructions, submissions and job activity can be clearly managed.

For Taskers

Taskers are people who complete available jobs on the platform.

A task may require an action such as visiting a website, following an instruction, interacting with supported platforms, answering a survey, testing something or completing another approved digital activity.

Taskers should always read the full task instructions before starting. Proof requirements may differ from one task to another.

For Posters

Posters are people, creators or businesses that need digital work completed.

Posters can create jobs with information such as the job title, description, instructions, task link, reward, available slots and proof requirements.

Jobs are designed to follow an organised funding, review and publication process before becoming available to Taskers.

Safety, Verification & Fairness

Trust is important to a task marketplace. NAIJA TASK is designed with systems intended to help reduce fake submissions, duplicate activity and misuse of the platform.

Depending on the task, proof may include screenshots or photographs, usernames, comments, links, answers or other information requested by the Poster.

Task activity and submissions can be reviewed before rewards are credited.

Important: Users should never provide passwords, authentication codes, banking PINs or other private security credentials as task proof.
