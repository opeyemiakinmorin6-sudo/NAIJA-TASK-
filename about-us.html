ABOUT NAIJA TASK

A clearer place to organise digital work.

NAIJA TASK is a Nigerian digital task marketplace. It connects people who want to complete online tasks with individuals and businesses that need clearly described digital work done.

WHY WE EXIST

Make the work clear before it begins.

Digital tasks can become confusing when instructions, proof requirements, submissions and feedback are scattered. NAIJA TASK brings those steps into one organised workflow so both sides can understand what is expected.

Our focus: understandable task details, visible rewards and requirements, organised proof submissions, account records and support.
🎯

Our mission

Make digital task opportunities and task management easier to access and understand for Nigerian individuals, creators and businesses.


🌱

Our direction

Keep improving the task experience, member tools, payment processes and support while being clear about what is currently available.

HOW IT WORKS

One platform, two sides.

Taskers complete jobs. Posters publish and manage them.

01 · JOIN

Create an account

Choose Tasker if you want to complete jobs, or Poster if you want to publish them.

02 · GET STARTED

Find or publish work

Taskers browse available jobs. Posters define the task, reward, quantity and proof requirements.

03 · DO THE WORK

Complete and submit

Taskers follow the instructions and submit the proof requested by the Poster.

04 · FOLLOW UP

Review and track

Posters review submissions. Taskers follow their submission and wallet records.

WHO WE SERVE

Built around real platform roles.

👤

For Taskers

Explore available tasks, check the reward and requirements, complete the work, submit proof and track the review outcome. Approved rewards are handled according to the applicable task and wallet rules.

🏢

For Posters

Create social or business tasks, explain what workers must do, set quantity and reward, complete the required funding and manage submitted proof.

TRANSPARENCY & SAFETY

Understand the rules. Protect your account.

Task availability, reward amounts, fees, approval decisions and withdrawal processing depend on the details shown in the platform and the applicable policies. Earnings are not guaranteed; they depend on available tasks and successful completion.

Account safety: Never share your password, one-time code, banking PIN or full card details with another member. Use the official Support Centre if something looks wrong.
