About the platform

What is NAIJA TASK?

NAIJA TASK is a Nigerian digital task marketplace. Taskers complete available online jobs and submit proof. Posters publish tasks and review the work submitted.

Who can use the platform?

People who want to complete tasks can register as Taskers. Individuals and businesses that want digital work completed can register as Posters. Follow the eligibility and account rules shown during registration.

Is every feature available all the time?

No. Some services depend on live integrations, account eligibility, admin settings and availability. Check the individual page for its current status and do not assume a feature is active just because it appears in the menu.

Taskers and task submissions

How do I start earning?

Sign in as a Tasker, open Find Tasks, choose a task you understand, complete it as described and submit the requested proof. Your reward depends on the review outcome and the rules for that task.

Why should I read the instructions before starting?

Each task can have different steps, eligibility rules and proof requirements. Missing a step or submitting unclear evidence can lead to rejection.

When will a reward appear in my wallet?

Check the task's status. A submitted task is not yet approved. The wallet is updated according to the task review and payment rules when the submission is approved.

What if my proof is rejected?

Read the rejection reason in My Task Activity. If you believe the decision does not match the task requirements, use Appeals where available or contact Support with the task details.

Posters and campaigns

How do I post a task?

Create a Poster account, open the Poster dashboard and choose the relevant form. Describe the task, add the correct link, set reward and quantity, define proof requirements and follow the payment steps shown.

What is the difference between a social task and a business task?

Social tasks are for clearly defined actions on supported digital/social platforms. Business tasks use the separate business form for work connected to a business or promotion. Choose the form that matches the job.

When will my task go live?

Publication can depend on successful payment, required funding, moderation and account settings. Check the status shown in your Poster dashboard.

How should I review submitted proof?

Compare the proof with the exact requirements you published. Approve work that meets those requirements and give a clear, factual reason when rejecting.

Wallet, payments and withdrawals

Where can I check my balance and transactions?

Open Wallet for your balance and Transactions for account records. If figures do not match, contact Support with the relevant transaction reference.

What is the minimum withdrawal and what fees apply?

These values can be changed by platform settings. Check the current Withdrawal page before submitting; the amount and any applicable fee should be shown there.

My payment is pending. Should I pay again?

No—do not immediately repeat a payment. Keep the transaction reference, check the payment status and contact Support if it does not update.

How long do withdrawals take?

Processing depends on the payment provider, bank and current platform processing. Check the status and reference in your account; contact Support if a transfer remains unresolved.

Account and safety

I cannot log in. What should I do?

Check that you are using the correct login page for your account type. Use Forgot password if needed. Taskers and Posters have separate login pages.

How do I contact NAIJA TASK?

Open the Support Centre and submit a request. Include the page or transaction reference involved, but never include your password, OTP or banking PIN.

What information should I never share?

Never share your password, one-time verification code, banking PIN, card security code or full card details with another member or as task proof.

Important: NAIJA TASK does not guarantee a fixed daily or monthly income. Task availability, reward amounts, fees and processing times can change. Always check the live details shown in your account.
