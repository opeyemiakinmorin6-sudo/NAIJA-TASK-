STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

Know what to do at every stage.

NAIJA TASK connects Taskers who complete digital jobs with Posters who publish them. Follow the guide for the account type you use.

For Taskers: find work and submit proof

Take time to read the full task before starting. The reward, remaining slots, instructions and proof requirements should guide your decision.

  1. Create a Tasker account. Register with details you can access and sign in.
  2. Open Find Tasks. Browse the tasks currently available to your account.
  3. Read the task details. Check the reward, slots, task link, deadline if shown, and exactly what proof is required.
  4. Complete the work. Follow the instructions honestly. Do not submit fake screenshots or claim work you did not do.
  5. Submit proof. Upload the requested evidence using the task submission page.
  6. Track the review. Check My Task Activity for the current status and any rejection reason.
  7. Check your wallet. When a submission is approved, confirm the credited amount and use the withdrawal option available to your account.
A submission is not the same as an approved task. Rewards depend on completing the requirements and the review outcome.

For Posters: publish and manage a task

A good task is specific. Tell workers what to do, where to do it, how many people you need and what evidence proves completion.

  1. Create a Poster account. Sign in to your Poster dashboard.
  2. Choose the correct form. Use the social task form for social-platform assignments or the business task form for business-related work.
  3. Write clear instructions. Add the correct destination link, task steps and proof requirements.
  4. Set reward and quantity. Review the total shown, including any applicable platform charges.
  5. Complete the required payment. Use the payment method displayed by the platform. A task should only become available when the required payment and approval checks are complete.
  6. Monitor submissions. Open your job management page and inspect the proof submitted by workers.
  7. Review fairly. Approve work that meets the stated requirements. If rejecting, provide a clear reason that matches the task instructions.
Do not ask workers for passwords, OTPs, bank PINs or other private credentials as proof. Do not create tasks that require fake reviews, spam or deceptive activity.

Wallet, payments and support

Wallet and withdrawals

Use Wallet and Transactions to check your records. Before withdrawing, check the current minimum, fees and available balance shown on the withdrawal page.

Payment questions

Keep the transaction reference for any payment. If a payment is pending or a balance looks wrong, contact Support rather than paying again immediately.

Rejected proof or disputes

Read the rejection reason first. If you believe a decision is incorrect, use the Appeals Centre where available and explain the issue clearly.

Account safety

Use a unique password and never share your OTP, password, banking PIN or full payment-card details.
