How payment works

  1. Choose an available listing and place an order.
  2. Read the payment instructions displayed for that order.
  3. Pay only to the officially confirmed account or method.
  4. Submit your payment reference and any required evidence through the approved payment process.
  5. Wait until an authorized administrator verifies the payment.
  6. The seller fulfils the order after the payment status is confirmed according to platform rules.

No bank account or transfer details are hardcoded into this page. Configure official payment instructions through your secure backend/admin settings.
