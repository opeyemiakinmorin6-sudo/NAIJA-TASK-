Campaign management

Transparent task requirements

State the exact steps and acceptable proof before people begin. Clear requirements help reduce confusion and disputes.

How to publish a task

  1. Create a Poster account and sign in to the Poster dashboard.
  2. Select the task form that matches the work you want completed.
  3. Write accurate instructions and provide the correct destination link.
  4. Set the quantity and review the total price displayed before payment.
  5. Complete the payment steps shown in the platform. Publication may depend on payment confirmation and moderation.
  6. Monitor submissions and review proof against the requirements you published.

Write a task people can understand

  • Describe one clear action at a time.
  • Include a working link and explain any eligibility conditions.
  • State what evidence is required and how it will be checked.
  • Set a realistic quantity and review the displayed campaign total.
  • Do not request passwords, OTPs, bank PINs, fake reviews, spam or deceptive engagement.
Important: Available task types, prices, platform fees and payment options can change. The live Poster dashboard and checkout display are the source of current details. Do not rely on old screenshots or third-party price lists.
