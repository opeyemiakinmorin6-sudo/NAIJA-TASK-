Before you get started

Do I need a Tasker or Poster account?

Choose Tasker if you want to complete jobs. Choose Poster if you want to create and fund jobs for others.

When do Taskers receive rewards?

Rewards are processed according to the task's review and approval process. Completing a task does not guarantee approval if its requirements are not met.

Can I use one login for both account types?

Use the login page matching the account type you registered. Poster accounts have a separate dashboard.

How can I get help?

Use the Support Centre from your account or contact Customer Care through the links on our About page.
