Frequently Asked Questions

Create a Tasker account, browse available tasks, read the instructions carefully and submit the required proof after completing a task.

Create a Poster account, provide the task details, set the reward and participant slots, then follow the funding and publishing steps shown on the platform.

A reward is credited after the submitted proof has been reviewed and approved according to the task requirements.

Eligible approved earnings appear in your wallet. You can request a withdrawal when you meet the current account and minimum withdrawal requirements displayed in your wallet.

Visit the Contact Us page or email naijataskofficia@gmail.com for help with your account or platform activity.
