A SIMPLE WORKFLOW

How NAIJA TASK works

Different goals, one straightforward process.

👤

For Taskers — complete tasks

  1. Create a Tasker account and sign in.
  2. Browse available tasks and check the reward, slots and requirements.
  3. Complete the task exactly as described.
  4. Submit the requested proof and follow its review status.
  5. When approved, check your wallet and use the withdrawal options available to your account.
See the Tasker guide →
🏢

For Posters — publish work

  1. Create a Poster account and open your dashboard.
  2. Choose a social task or business task form.
  3. Set the instructions, reward, quantity and proof requirements.
  4. Review the displayed price and complete the required payment.
  5. Track submissions and review the proof received.
See the Poster guide →

Task availability, approval times, fees and withdrawal processing depend on the task rules and the current account settings shown on the platform. Completing a task does not guarantee approval if the instructions are not met.

QUICK ANSWERS

Questions people ask

Understand the basics before you create an account.

What is NAIJA TASK?

It is a digital task marketplace connecting Taskers who complete jobs with Posters who publish them.

How do I receive a task reward?

Complete the task as described and submit the required proof. Rewards are credited according to the review outcome and the applicable task rules.

Can I post a task for my business?

Yes. Create a Poster account and choose the social task or business task option. Review the task details and displayed charges before payment.

Are all tasks always available?

No. Available tasks and slots change as jobs are published, filled, paused or completed.

What if my proof is rejected?

Read the reason shown with the submission. If you believe the decision is wrong, use the Appeals Centre or contact Support where available.

Where can I get help?

Visit the Support Centre or read the full FAQ and step-by-step guide.
