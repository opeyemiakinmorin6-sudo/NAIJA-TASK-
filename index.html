Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you create an account or publish a task.

How do tasks work?

Posters publish a task with instructions, eligibility and a reward. Taskers choose available tasks, complete the stated requirements and submit the requested proof for review.

How do I earn?

Create a Tasker account, review available tasks and their rewards, complete the instructions and submit proof. Rewards are credited after the submission is approved.

How do I get paid?

Approved task rewards appear in your NAIJA TASK wallet. Withdrawal options, minimums and applicable fees are shown in the wallet and withdrawal flow.

Can I post my own task?

Yes. Register through the Poster sign-up flow, then use your Poster workspace to create a campaign, set its instructions, audience, slots and budget.

How is proof checked?

The Poster reviews the proof submitted against the task instructions and approves or rejects the submission. Only approved submissions qualify for the task reward.
