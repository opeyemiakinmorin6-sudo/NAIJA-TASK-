Frequently Asked Questions

Need more help? Our support team can help you find your way around the platform.

How do tasks work?

Posters create a task with instructions, a reward and available slots. Taskers choose a suitable task, complete the required steps and submit proof for review.

How do I earn?

Create a Tasker account, browse available tasks, read each task's requirements and submit genuine proof when you finish. Rewards are credited for approved submissions.

How do I get paid?

Approved task rewards appear in your NAIJA TASK wallet. You can request a withdrawal when your account meets the current withdrawal requirements shown in the wallet.

Can I post my own task?

Yes. Create a Poster account, prepare your task instructions and reward, then follow the posting and funding steps shown in your account.

How is proof checked?

Proof is reviewed against the task instructions. A Poster or authorised reviewer checks whether the submitted work meets the stated requirements before approval.
