QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

What is NAIJA TASK?

NAIJA TASK is a digital task platform connecting people who want to complete online jobs with people who want to publish campaigns.

Should I register as a Tasker or Poster?

Choose Tasker if you want to complete available jobs. Choose Poster if you want to publish and manage campaigns.

When are task rewards credited?

Rewards follow the task's review and approval process. A submission must meet the stated requirements before it can be approved.

How do I get help?

Use the Support Centre from your account or visit the About page for the platform's contact information.
