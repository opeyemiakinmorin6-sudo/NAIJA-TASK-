How to get started

1. Register

Create a Tasker account using details you can access. Keep your password private and use the correct Tasker sign-in page.

2. Browse available tasks

Open the task area and review the task title, reward, remaining slots, instructions and proof requirements before you begin.

3. Complete the instructions

Carry out the requested work honestly. Do not use fake engagement, misleading activity or another person's account.

4. Submit proof and track review

Upload the evidence requested by the Poster. A submission must be reviewed; submitting proof does not automatically mean it has been approved.

What kinds of tasks might be available?

Task categories and availability can change. Depending on what Posters publish and what the platform currently supports, tasks may involve defined digital actions, content engagement or other online assignments. Always check the live task details rather than relying on an old example or a promised amount.

Understand rewards and withdrawals

Each task should show its reward and requirements before you start. Rewards are subject to successful completion and review. Check your account's wallet and withdrawal page for the current balance, eligibility, minimum withdrawal and any applicable fees. NAIJA TASK does not promise a fixed income or guaranteed daily earnings.

Stay safe: Never share your password, OTP, bank PIN or card security details. Do not pay an unofficial person to unlock a task or release a reward. Use the platform's official support channel for account or payment problems.
