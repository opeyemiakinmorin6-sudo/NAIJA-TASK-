About NAIJA TASK

NAIJA TASK connects users with available tasks, jobs and digital opportunities. Rewards are credited after required proof has been reviewed and approved according to platform rules.

How it works

  • Create and verify your account.
  • Activate your account when required.
  • Browse available tasks.
  • Follow the task instructions.
  • Upload the required photo proof.
  • Submit the proof for review.
  • Approved rewards are credited to your wallet.
