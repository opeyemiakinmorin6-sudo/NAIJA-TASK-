About NAIJA TASK

NAIJA TASK is a platform designed to connect users with available tasks, jobs and digital opportunities. Users can complete eligible tasks, submit the required proof and receive rewards when their submissions are reviewed and approved.

How NAIJA TASK Works

  • Create and verify your NAIJA TASK account.
  • Activate your account when activation is required.
  • Browse available tasks or jobs.
  • Follow the instructions provided by the task owner.
  • Submit the required proof.
  • Wait for review and approval.
  • Approved rewards are reflected according to platform rules.
